EiBi broadcast schedule database
================================

The bundled EIBI.DAT database is derived from the official public EiBi
shortwave broadcast schedule compiled by Eike Bierwirth.

Source: https://www.eibispace.de/
Schedule source date: October 4, 2026
Bundled database: 9,541 records, 286,230 bytes
SHA-256: 4bfaa94c713d660445b92672b463620ab23f2f3a11dc306e27e3759f88191f64
